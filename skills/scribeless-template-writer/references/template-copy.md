# Template Copy Guidance

## Ecommerce Thank-You

Hi {{first_name}},

Thanks so much for your recent order. We really appreciate you choosing us, and I hope you love it when it arrives.

As a small thank-you, scan the QR code for something we saved for returning customers.

Best,
{{sender_name}}

## B2B Follow-Up

Hi {{first_name}},

It was good learning more about {{company}}. I thought a handwritten note would stand out more than another email.

If it is useful, scan the QR code and pick a time that works for a follow-up.

Best,
{{sender_name}}

## Winback

Hi {{first_name}},

It has been a little while since we last heard from you, so I wanted to send a quick note.

We would love to welcome you back. Scan the QR code to see what is new.

Best,
{{sender_name}}

## QA Checklist

- Variables exactly match recipient data keys.
- The first line still works if the recipient only has a first name.
- The CTA is specific and easy to complete.
- The tone matches the sender relationship.
- The copy does not rely on unsupported discounts, guarantees, or claims.
- The QR destination and UTM tracking are ready before sending.
