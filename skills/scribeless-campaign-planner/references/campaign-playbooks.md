# Campaign Playbooks

## Ecommerce and Retail

- Post-purchase thank-you: send after first order or high-value order; personalize with product/category and sender.
- VIP retention: send to high-LTV customers before a launch, sale, or anniversary.
- Winback: send after inactivity; use a specific reason to return and a clear QR destination.
- Review/referral: ask for review or referral after successful delivery; keep tone grateful, not transactional.

## B2B and SaaS

- ABM opener: small targeted account list; mention company, role, or pain point.
- Demo no-show or follow-up: send after missed or completed demo; CTA to book next step.
- Renewal or expansion: send to champions and stakeholders before decision window.
- Event follow-up: send within days of meeting; include exact event and next action.

## Real Estate

- Listing acquisition: target homeowners or past leads; lead with local relevance and trust.
- Post-valuation follow-up: reference the interaction and next step.
- Past-client referral: thank them and make referral request easy.

## Home Services

- Quote follow-up: send after quote or estimate; emphasize trust, timing, and next booking step.
- Seasonal reminder: send before high-demand windows.
- Reactivation: target old customers with maintenance or upgrade prompt.

## Financial Services

- Onboarding welcome: send after account opening or consultation.
- Annual review prompt: use a personal check-in tone and compliant CTA.
- Referral request: send to satisfied clients with light wording and review requirement.

## Nonprofit

- Donor thank-you: send soon after donation; reference impact.
- Lapsed donor reactivation: acknowledge prior support and show current need.
- Event follow-up: thank attendees and direct to next action.

## Measurement

- Track QR scans when the CTA is digital.
- Use unique QR destinations or UTM parameters per campaign.
- Compare response rate by audience, message angle, timing, and product.
